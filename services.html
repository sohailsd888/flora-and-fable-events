FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Planning Clarity.

Answers to essential questions before we begin designing your celebration.

I How far in advance should we reserve Flora & Fable?
+
For full destination weddings across Italy, France, and royal heritage estates in India, we recommend inquiring 12 to 18 months ahead to secure premier palatial estates. Intimate weekend affairs and couture day direction can occasionally be accommodated within 6 months.
II Do you orchestrate celebrations outside Europe?
+
Yes. Our directors have orchestrated private celebrations across 28 countries including India, Japan, Switzerland, Greece, Morocco, and private Caribbean archipelagos, collaborating with trusted global aviation and security partners.
III How does the interactive Couples Portal work?
+
Upon retainer, you receive private credentials to our online Couples Suite where you can track 22 bespoke milestones, approve floral architectural schematics, review signed vendor contracts, and view transparent installment statements anytime.
