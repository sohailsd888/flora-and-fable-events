THE CELESTIA APPROACH

Spaces Transformed Into Living Poetry.

At CELESTIA, decor is never an afterthought or a collection of generic centerpieces. Our spatial directors collaborate with structural engineers, lighting designers, and botanical sculptors to transform historic rooms and gardens into immersive sensory worlds.

Every concept begins with hand-rendered watercolors, tactile Italian silk and linen swatches, and exact 3D CAD floor plans so you can walk through your ceremony and reception months before a single flower is cut.
